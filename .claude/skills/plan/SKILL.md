## What this does

One job: decide today's three priorities and put them in today's note.

## Building the list

Draw on, in order:

1. the **inbox** brief if it ran today — those items already passed a cost-of-ignoring test
2. yesterday's `## Priorities` in the vault, read through the **vault** skill — anything unfinished carries over and gets named as a carry-over
3. whatever I say in the moment, which outranks both

Each priority is one line: a verb, the thing, and what done looks like. "Send Rael the revised scope, by 3pm" — not "scope work".

Three is the cap. A fourth genuinely pressing thing displaces one, and I get told which one it pushed out and why.

## Confirming

Show me the three before anything is written and wait for a yes. Planning my day without me agreeing to the plan defeats the point.

## Writing

Write through the **vault** skill, under `## Priorities` in `Daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` for today's date.

If that section already exists, show me the current three and the proposed three and let me choose — never silently replace a plan I already made. Nothing is written anywhere outside the vault.

## Afterwards

Confirm in one line what was written and to which file. No restating the list back at me — it's in the note.
